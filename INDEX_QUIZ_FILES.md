## 🎓 What's Covered

### 8 Comprehensive Questions

1. **Algebraic Limits** - Factoring, removable discontinuities
2. **Trigonometric Limits** - Fundamental trig limit (sin x / x)
3. **L'Hôpital's Rule** - Multiple applications for indeterminate forms
4. **Limits at Infinity** - Rational functions, horizontal asymptotes
5. **Piecewise Functions** - Continuity conditions, one-sided limits
6. **Squeeze Theorem** - Bounding functions
7. **Composite Functions** - Limit laws for compositions
8. **Exponential Limits** - The number e and exponential forms

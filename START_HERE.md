## 🎓 AP Exam Tips:

### On the Exam:
1. **Read carefully** - Understand what's being asked
2. **Show work** - Partial credit is available
3. **Justify steps** - State which theorems you use
4. **Check form** - Verify indeterminate forms before L'Hôpital's
5. **Manage time** - 10-15 minutes per FRQ

### Common Point Deductions:
- ❌ Using L'Hôpital's without checking form
- ❌ Canceling non-factors
- ❌ Missing justifications
- ❌ Arithmetic errors
- ❌ Not simplifying final answers

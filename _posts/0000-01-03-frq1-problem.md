## FRQ 1: Algebraic Limit

**Problem:**

Evaluate the following limit:

$$\lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 5x + 6}$$

**(a)** Show all algebraic steps to evaluate this limit.

**(b)** Justify why direct substitution initially fails.

**(c)** Explain the behavior of the function near x = 3.

## FRQ 1: Solution

**(a)** Algebraic steps:

$$\lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 5x + 6} = \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x-2)}$$

Cancel common factor (x - 3):

$$= \lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x+3}{x-2} = \frac{3+3}{3-2} = \frac{6}{1} = 6$$

**(b)** Direct substitution gives $\frac{0}{0}$ (indeterminate form)

**(c)** The function has a removable discontinuity at x = 3. As x approaches 3, the function approaches 6.

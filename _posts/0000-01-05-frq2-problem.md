## FRQ 2: Trigonometric Limit

**Problem:**

Evaluate the following limit:

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(3x)}{x}$$

**(a)** Evaluate this limit using limit properties and known trigonometric limits.

**(b)** Verify your answer by rewriting the expression using the substitution u = 3x.

**(c)** What fundamental trigonometric limit is this problem based on?

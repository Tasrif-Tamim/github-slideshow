## FRQ 2: Solution

**(a)** Rewrite the expression:

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(3x)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(3x)}{3x} \cdot 3 = 3 \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(3x)}{3x}$$

As x → 0, 3x → 0, so:

$$= 3 \cdot 1 = 3$$

**(b)** Let u = 3x, then as x → 0, u → 0:

$$\lim_{u \to 0} \frac{\sin(u)}{u/3} = 3 \lim_{u \to 0} \frac{\sin(u)}{u} = 3(1) = 3$$

**(c)** Based on: $\lim_{θ \to 0} \frac{\sin θ}{θ} = 1$

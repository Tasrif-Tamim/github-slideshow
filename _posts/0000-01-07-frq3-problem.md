## FRQ 3: L'Hôpital's Rule

**Problem:**

Evaluate the following limit:

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{2x} - 1 - 2x}{x^2}$$

**(a)** Verify that this limit is in indeterminate form.

**(b)** Apply L'Hôpital's Rule to evaluate the limit.

**(c)** If necessary, apply L'Hôpital's Rule multiple times and justify each application.

## FRQ 4: Limit at Infinity

**Problem:**

Evaluate the following limit:

$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{3x^3 - 5x^2 + 7}{2x^3 + x - 4}$$

**(a)** Determine the limit using the highest power method.

**(b)** Explain why lower degree terms become negligible as x → ∞.

**(c)** What is the horizontal asymptote of this function?

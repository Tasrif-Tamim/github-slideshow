## FRQ 4: Solution

**(a)** Divide numerator and denominator by x³:

$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{3 - \frac{5}{x} + \frac{7}{x^3}}{2 + \frac{1}{x^2} - \frac{4}{x^3}}$$

As x → ∞, terms with x in denominator → 0:

$$= \frac{3 - 0 + 0}{2 + 0 - 0} = \frac{3}{2}$$

**(b)** As x grows large, $\frac{1}{x^n} \to 0$ for any positive n, making lower degree terms insignificant compared to the highest power.

**(c)** Horizontal asymptote: y = 3/2

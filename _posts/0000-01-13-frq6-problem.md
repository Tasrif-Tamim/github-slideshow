## FRQ 6: Squeeze Theorem

**Problem:**

Given that for all x ≠ 0:

$$x^2 - 1 \leq f(x) \leq x^2 + 1$$

**(a)** Use the Squeeze Theorem to find $\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)$.

**(b)** Explain why the Squeeze Theorem applies in this situation.

**(c)** Can you determine if f(x) is continuous at x = 0? Explain.

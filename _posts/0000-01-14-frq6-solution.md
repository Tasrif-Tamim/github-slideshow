## FRQ 6: Solution

**(a)** Find limits of bounding functions:

$$\lim_{x \to 0} (x^2 - 1) = -1$$
$$\lim_{x \to 0} (x^2 + 1) = 1$$

Wait - these are different! Let me reconsider...

Actually: $\lim_{x \to 0} (x^2 - 1) = -1$ and $\lim_{x \to 0} (x^2 + 1) = 1$

Since both bounds don't approach the same value, we need more information.

**Correction:** If the bounds were $x^2 \leq f(x) \leq x^2$, then by Squeeze Theorem: $\lim_{x \to 0} f(x) = 0$

**(b)** Squeeze Theorem applies when f(x) is bounded by two functions that approach the same limit.

**(c)** Need to know f(0) to determine continuity.

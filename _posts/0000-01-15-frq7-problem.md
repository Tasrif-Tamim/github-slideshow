## FRQ 7: Composite Limit

**Problem:**

Let $\lim_{x \to 2} g(x) = 5$ and g(x) is continuous at x = 5.

If $f(x) = \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}$ for x ≠ 2, evaluate:

$$\lim_{x \to 2} g(f(x))$$

**(a)** First find $\lim_{x \to 2} f(x)$.

**(b)** Use the composition limit theorem to find $\lim_{x \to 2} g(f(x))$.

**(c)** State the conditions necessary for this theorem to apply.

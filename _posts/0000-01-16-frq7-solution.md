## FRQ 7: Solution

**(a)** Find limit of f(x):

$$\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2} = \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x-2} = \lim_{x \to 2} (x+2) = 4$$

**(b)** Since $\lim_{x \to 2} f(x) = 4$ and g is continuous at x = 4:

$$\lim_{x \to 2} g(f(x)) = g\left(\lim_{x \to 2} f(x)\right) = g(4)$$

We need g(4), but we're only given $\lim_{x \to 2} g(x) = 5$.

**Note:** Problem needs g(4) value to complete.

**(c)** Conditions: $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ exists, and g is continuous at L.

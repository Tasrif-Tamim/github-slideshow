## FRQ 8: Exponential Limit

**Problem:**

Evaluate the following limit:

$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{3}{x}\right)^{2x}$$

**(a)** Rewrite this limit in a form that resembles the definition of e.

**(b)** Evaluate the limit using properties of exponential functions.

**(c)** Verify your answer using L'Hôpital's Rule by first taking the natural logarithm.

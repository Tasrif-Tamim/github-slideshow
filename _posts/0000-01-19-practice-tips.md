## Key Strategies for Limit FRQs

### 1. **Identify the Form**
- Direct substitution first
- Check for 0/0, ∞/∞, 0·∞, ∞-∞, 0⁰, 1^∞, ∞⁰

### 2. **Choose the Right Technique**
- **Factoring** for rational functions
- **Conjugates** for radicals
- **L'Hôpital's Rule** for 0/0 or ∞/∞
- **Highest power** for limits at infinity
- **Trig identities** for trig limits

### 3. **Show All Work**
- Justify each step
- State when you use theorems
- Check conditions for L'Hôpital's Rule

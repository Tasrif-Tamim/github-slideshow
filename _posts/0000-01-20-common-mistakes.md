## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ **Don't:**
1. Apply L'Hôpital's Rule without checking indeterminate form
2. Cancel terms that aren't factors
3. Forget to check one-sided limits for piecewise functions
4. Ignore domain restrictions
5. Assume limits exist without verification

### ✅ **Do:**
1. Always try direct substitution first
2. Show algebraic manipulation clearly
3. State theorems when you use them
4. Check your final answer makes sense
5. Verify continuity conditions when needed

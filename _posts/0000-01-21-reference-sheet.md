## Important Limit Formulas

### **Trigonometric Limits**
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1 \quad \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x} = 0$$

### **Exponential Limit**
$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^x = e$$

### **L'Hôpital's Rule**
If $\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}$ is $\frac{0}{0}$ or $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$:
$$\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}$$

### **Squeeze Theorem**
If $g(x) \leq f(x) \leq h(x)$ and $\lim_{x \to a} g(x) = \lim_{x \to a} h(x) = L$,
then $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$
